# Brand

## Workflow

1. Locate authoritative brand guidelines, existing product language, logos, tokens, and approved assets.
2. Separate established facts from proposed choices.
3. Define only what the task needs: audience, promise, voice traits, message hierarchy, visual cues, and usage constraints.
4. Apply those decisions consistently to the requested artifact or interface.
5. Check terminology, tone, contrast, legibility, asset rights, and consistency with existing surfaces.

## Boundaries

- Do not replace an existing identity without an explicit redesign request.
- Do not generate logos, images, or banners automatically; route asset creation to the appropriate tool only when requested.
- Do not treat speculative brand direction as approved policy.
- Keep product engineering details out of user-facing brand copy.
