# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Protect private information and give credit for work reused from others.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, disclosure of private information, plagiarism, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces managed for this project. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through a private contact method published on their GitHub profiles. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose a target or sensitive evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be considered as privately and impartially as the available information allows.
